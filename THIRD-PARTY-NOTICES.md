# Third-party notices

mshmsh-visual-design (formerly designa-xoshaka) is written in its own words, but it learned from these projects, and adapts some of their ideas, values and data. Thank you to their authors.

| Project | Author | Licence | What was adapted |
|---|---|---|---|
| [impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) | Paul Bakaus | Apache-2.0 | The usability critique scored /40 with bands and P0-P3 severity, the technical audit /20, surface modes (persuade/operate/read/experience), the identity lock and six variant axes, colour strategies, "question last" mechanics, the craft floor |
| [taste-skill](https://github.com/leonxlnx/taste-skill) | Leonxlnx | MIT | Dealing directions at random with a history, the client inventory and idea sentence with the competitor test, "one live thing", the fluid type scale, layout and imagery numbers, app-screen rules, the dated waves of AI-look tells |
| [skills](https://github.com/emilkowalski/skills) | Emil Kowalski | MIT | The frequency-of-use gate for motion, easing curves and duration table, component motion recipes, enter/exit asymmetry, worst-case data testing, the option-picker principles |
| [web-interface-guidelines](https://github.com/vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines) | Vercel Labs | MIT | The interface quality checklist (accessibility, focus, forms, touch, content, images, typography, theming, i18n) |
| [ui-ux-pro-max-skill](https://github.com/nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill) | Next Level Builder | MIT | Palettes by product type, type pairings, the list of Arabic-script Google fonts, page patterns and print identity styles, condensed in `references/catalogs.md` |
| unslop-ui | (installed skill, no licence file) | n/a | "A tell is a default nobody chose", the cream + serif + sage default, the ignore marker for deliberate choices |
| [frontend-design](https://github.com/anthropics/skills) | Anthropic | Apache-2.0 | The calibration list of AI-generated design clusters, two-pass plan-then-build, restraint ("remove one accessory") |
| [claude-skills](https://github.com/rampstackco/claude-skills) | RampStack Co. | MIT | Logo architectures and the primary/compact/smallest fallback set, the application-context tests (favicon, app icon, embroidery, one colour, reversed, signage), the restraint tests, the logo hand-off package and naming, the illustration brief fields (`identity.md`, `illustration.md`) |
| [brand-building-skills](https://github.com/arnabbagxd/brand-building-skills) | arnabbagxd | MIT | Packaging's jobs, the information hierarchy by face, the fixed-versus-changing split for product ranges (`packaging.md`) |
| [alterlab-fc-skills](https://github.com/alterlab-ieu/alterlab-fc-skills) | Cem İpek / AlterLab Creative Technologies Laboratory | MIT | The shelf tests and dieline line types for packaging; reading levels by seconds, one focal point and format adaptation for posters (`packaging.md`, `graphic.md`) |
| [skills-design](https://github.com/poorvith-mp/skills-design) | Poorvith M P | MIT | The dieline as an overprinted spot-colour layer, rich black for floods and 100% K for small text, dielines always from the manufacturer (`packaging.md`) |
| [agent-loom](https://github.com/dvy1987/agent-loom) | dvy1987 | MIT | Static SVG craft: viewBox fill, few paths and primitives, one stroke weight per set, SVGO with prefixIds, cleaning traced bitmaps (`illustration.md`) |

Facts and figures (not licensed content) were checked against their published sources in November 2026: GS1 General Specifications and GS1 member organisations' guides (EAN-13 sizes, quiet zones, placement), EU Regulation 1169/2011 Article 13 (minimum x-height), Instagram's and Meta's guidance (post sizes, safe zones), Canva's help centre (print export), and trade guides on Iraq's labelling requirements (COSQC). They change; the files say where to check.
