# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (2026-11-20)

Extended to brand identity, packaging, illustration and graphic design after studying the open skills for each, then renamed from designa-xoshaka to mshmsh-visual-design.

- Brand identity (`references/identity.md`): the brief, mark architectures with a primary, compact and favicon-grade mark, Kurdish and Latin lockups designed together, drawing marks in SVG, the tests every mark passes, colour values for screen and print, guidelines and the hand-off package.
- Packaging (`references/packaging.md`): the converter's dieline and its layers, the front and the shelf tests, ranges, the legal information (including Iraq's Arabic labelling), barcodes at GS1 size, inks, white ink and finishes, Kurdish on packs, mockups and preflight.
- Illustration (`references/illustration.md`): the style sheet, ten style families, ways to produce it, SVG craft, characters, local motifs and patterns, rules for generated images.
- Graphic pieces (`references/graphic.md`): one message and three reading levels, grids and mirrored reading paths, type for distance and phones, 2026 social sizes and safe zones, templates, and making it in code, Canva or Figma.
- Free resources (`references/resources.md`): tools, fonts, icons, illustration and photo sources with their licences, packaging tools, references and learning.
- New tools: `scripts/ean13.py` (checks a GTIN and draws its EAN-13 at exact size), `assets/logo-sheet.html` (every logo test on one sheet), `assets/pack-mockup.html` (3D box and jar from flat artwork).
- `scripts/deal.py` deals directions for identities, packs, illustration and graphic pieces too (41 new directions).
- Review tells and ruts for logos, packs, illustration and posters; a bigger DESIGN.md template; four new test tasks in `evals/`.
- Tests in `tests/`, run by GitHub on every push; a release zip for the Claude app on every change to main.
