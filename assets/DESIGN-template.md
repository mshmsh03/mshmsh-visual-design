# DESIGN.md

The design contract for this project. Every decision lands here. Read it before changing anything, and add to it after every pick. Delete the sections the project doesn't use (a logo job has no Tokens table; a website has no Packaging section).

## Brief
- **For:** who uses it, where, on what device or in what place (shelf, wall, hand, phone feed)
- **The job:** what a visitor, customer or user must be able to do first
- **Brand material:** logo files, style sheet, fonts, colours, existing printed pieces, old site, product photos (paths)
- **Reference:** a real site, piece or place whose feel to follow
- **Languages:** English / Kurdish Sorani (`ckb`, right-to-left) / Arabic (`ar`, right-to-left); which one leads
- **Digits:** Western (0-9) or Eastern (٠-٩), per language
- **Devices and sizes:** phone 390, desktop 1440, till 1366x768, print size and bleed, social sizes
- **Made in:** code (HTML/CSS/SVG to PDF and PNG), Canva, Figma, or the person's own tool
- **Tone in three words:** (and three "this, not that" pairs for identities)
- **Constraints:** stack, printer, deadline, what must not change
